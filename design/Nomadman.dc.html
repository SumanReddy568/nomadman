About

I take the long way around.

I started carrying a camera because I kept forgetting the good parts. Nine years later the forgetting is under control and the archive is not. This site is my attempt at giving the better frames somewhere to live, with the notes I wrote next to them.

I shoot digital for work and film when I am travelling for myself. Mostly one body, one 35mm lens. If a frame needed a second lens, it probably needed a second trip instead.
